About me

My name is Erik Vaněk and I am an experience designer from Brno, Czechia.

With a blend of technical expertise and creative problem-solving, I’ve helped organisations from startups through public services to cultural institutions create human-centred services that resonate with the people who use them.

When I work with clients, I start by understanding their needs and goals, because getting aligned is the key to success. Once we are, we move on to empathising with their own clients. Good solutions only emerge from fully understanding those needs and constraints.

Outside of work I cook, ferment and build aeroponic systems. I like experimenting with new recipes and techniques, from kombucha and tepache to kimchi.

How I work

  • Empathy. Understanding the people involved is what makes a solution resonate and deliver results.
  • Fast feedback. Design, test and refine in quick rounds, again and again.
  • Effective collaboration. Smarter ways of working than group chats and long email chains.
  • Stakeholder alignment. A shared vision unites the effort and makes ambitious goals reachable.
  • Agile principles. Small steps and continuous improvement get you to great things faster.
