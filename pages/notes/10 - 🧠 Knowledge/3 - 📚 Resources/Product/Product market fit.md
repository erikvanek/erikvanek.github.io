## Three generations to fit - *Build* (Fadell)

- *typically 3 generations of a disruptive product need to be built to get it right, find the product-market fit & make profit and the main reason for this is that learning takes time + also the fact that most customers are not early adopters complicate things* ([[Build#^build-three-generations|Fadell]])
- **the rule of thumb of creating great products / companies: 1. you build the product, 2. you fix the product, 3. you build the business** ([[Build#^build-rule-of-thumb|Fadell]])
- from Crossing the Chasm - 3 phases with typical customer types: V1 - innovators & early adopters; V2 - early adopters & early majority; V3 - late majority & laggards ([[Build#^build-chasm-phases|Fadell]])
- *focusing on profit makes sense only after finding appropriate product-market fit* - hardware companies look at COGS, software companies at CAC ([[Build#^build-profit-after-pmf|Fadell]])
- **products are like humans - before running, they need to learn to crawl and then to walk** ([[Build#^build-products-like-humans|Fadell]])
