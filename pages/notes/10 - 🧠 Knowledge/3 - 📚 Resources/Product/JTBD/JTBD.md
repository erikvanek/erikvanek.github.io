# Jobs To Be Done

**JTBD is a theory** about why people buy and use what they do: a job is the **progress an individual is trying to make in a particular circumstance**. People don't buy products, they hire them to make progress - and they fire them when something makes better progress.

Its practical value is stability. Jobs change far more slowly than the technologies and solutions that serve them, which makes them a firmer foundation for [[Product Vision|vision]] and [[Product Strategy|strategy]] than any current solution set.

The full write-up lives in [[04 - 💽 RAW/2025/04/06/JTBD|Jobs To Be Done Framework]]. This note is the hub for the concepts around it.

## The pieces

- [[Motivation]] - the forces that push and pull someone toward a switch
- [[Value]] - what the job is worth to the person doing it
- [[Value proposition]] - how an offer speaks to a job
- [[Competition]] - anything hired for the same job, not just the same category
- [[Growth]] - jobs as the engine of growth
- [[Measuring]] - measuring against jobs rather than features
- [[Activity-centered design]] - designing around what people are doing

## Where it connects

- [[Problem framing]] - a job is one of the sharpest lenses for framing a problem
- [[Value proposition canvas]] - the canvas that operationalises jobs, pains and gains
- [[Insight]] - jobs surface the "why" behind observed behaviour
- [[Innovation]] - anticipating the next struggle after the current one is sorted
