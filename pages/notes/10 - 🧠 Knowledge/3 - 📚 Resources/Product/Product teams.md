- focus on solving problems, **not implementing features**

## Outcome-Based Teams
- teams should focus on outcomes (behavior changes that drive business results) rather than outputs (features)
- teams need a theory: if we create a certain impact for the customer, it will yield a certain outcome for the business
- output-driven teams give an illusion of control because they work with fixed feature sets and deadlines
- **finished feature ≠ value delivery**
- a core challenge: teams are often organized around making stuff, making it hard to focus on cross-functional outcomes
- trust is a key ingredient for adopting an outcome-oriented approach
- earlier engagement between designers and developers is critical to orient work more around outcomes

## Good vs. bad product managers — *The Hard Thing About Hard Things* (Horowitz)
- *good product managers crisply define the target, the "what" and manage the delivery of the "what", they don't give direction informally but they gather information informally* ([[The Hard Thing About Hard Things#^hthat-good-pm|Horowitz]])
- *good product managers anticipate product flaws and build real solutions, bad PMs firefight all day* ([[The Hard Thing About Hard Things#^hthat-pm-anticipate|Horowitz]])
- *good product managers decompose problems, bad ones mold problems into one* ([[The Hard Thing About Hard Things#^hthat-pm-decompose|Horowitz]])

## The PM as producer - *Build* (Fadell)

- *building products is like making songs and the product team is the band and the PM is the producer and they should be **rarely** dictating everything, rather than that they should be **empowering** the team - if a PM makes most of important decisions, it's not a good PM* ([[Build#^build-pm-producer|Fadell]])
- *the superpower of every great product manager is empathy* ([[Build#^build-pm-empathy|Fadell]])
- *product people are the hardest to hire and to train and that's why they are so valuable* ([[Build#^build-pm-hardest-hire|Fadell]])
- *product messaging shapes the product - what you're telling to customers is what you're building* ([[Build#^build-messaging-shapes|Fadell]])
- *during hiring it's important that during interview process - candidates talk to roles they will be working with e.g. PMs to designers or QAs with engineers* ([[Build#^build-interview-cross-roles|Fadell]])
