## Avoiding habituation - *Build* (Fadell)

- *a good principle to follow as a designer is to avoid habituation - e.g. it's easy to get used to brand stickers on apples - that moment of getting used to something is dangerous* ([[Build#^build-habituation|Fadell]])
- **you can't solve interesting problems if you don't notice they're out there and that's a difference between a startup founder and its employee - e.g. before iPod every was ok / habituated to take their CD stashes with them** ([[Build#^build-notice-problems|Fadell]])
- **as a designer you can find improvement to experiences where people in their day to day just expect them to be terrible** ([[Build#^build-expect-terrible|Fadell]])
- designing means thinking through a problem and finding an elegant solution and being a designer means making things work better ([[Build#^build-designing-means|Fadell]])
- *there are no perfect designs and there are always constraints* ([[Build#^build-no-perfect-design|Fadell]])
- *"not everyone can be a designer but everyone can think like one"* ([[Build#^build-think-like-designer|Fadell]])
