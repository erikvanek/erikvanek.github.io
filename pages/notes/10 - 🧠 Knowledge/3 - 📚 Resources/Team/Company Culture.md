- great example of how they do it at [Duolingo](https://handbook.duolingo.com/)
- *in a sign of good culture - good news travel slow and bad news spread fast → what helps is to encourage - not punish - people to put problems in the open* ([[The Hard Thing About Hard Things#^hthat-good-news|Horowitz]])
- being a good company does not matter when things go well but matters big time when things don't go so well which happens at some point for sure ([[The Hard Thing About Hard Things#^hthat-good-company|Horowitz]])
- **people won't remember everything working for your organization but they will remember the day of being laid off - that's why it's important that their direct managers lay them off and not HR or something similar as they don't have that strong relationships with those being laid off** ([[The Hard Thing About Hard Things#^hthat-layoffs|Horowitz]])

## Silos, breakpoints & change - *Build* (Fadell)

- first hires are crucial for setting a company culture ([[Build#^build-first-hires|Fadell]])
- *silos start appearing when first management layer is introduced and people distribute into verticals & those might not share enough* ([[Build#^build-silos|Fadell]])
- *lack of transparency due to the lack of bottom-up / top-down information flow can bring distrust - when there's a lack of data, distrust fills that void* ([[Build#^build-distrust-void|Fadell]])
- *culture is the hardest thing to pinpoint and to preserve* ([[Build#^build-culture-hardest|Fadell]])
- *org breakpoints seem to catch (leadership) people off guard and it's annoying to plan for them because it's messy - sadly here the "if it's not broken, don't fix it" does not work* ([[Build#^build-breakpoints-offguard|Fadell]])
- coaching / mentoring is especially important at breakpoints - e.g. when around 30-40 ppl or 80-120 ([[Build#^build-breakpoint-sizes|Fadell]])
- **in the process of change - don't worry what you're about to lose - think of what you want to become** ([[Build#^build-what-to-become|Fadell]])
