What designing is

  • designing means thinking through a problem and finding an elegant solution and being a designer means making things work better (Fadell)
  • "not everyone can be a designer but everyone can think like one" (Fadell)

Ship rough

  • be shitty, not pretty
  • there are no perfect designs and there are always constraints (Fadell)

Noticing what everyone else stopped seeing

  • a good principle to follow as a designer is to avoid habituation - e.g. it's easy to get used to brand stickers on apples - that moment of getting used to something is dangerous (Fadell)
  • you can't solve interesting problems if you don't notice they're out there and that's a difference between a startup founder and its employee - e.g. before iPod every was ok / habituated to take their CD stashes with them (Fadell)
  • as a designer you can find improvement to experiences where people in their day to day just expect them to be terrible (Fadell)
